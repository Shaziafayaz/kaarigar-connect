About Kaarigar Connect

Kaarigar Connect is a platform that connects customers with skilled workers for everyday services and home repairs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make it easier for customers to find skilled workers and for workers to showcase their skills, experience, and services. We aim to bring both together on one platform, making it easier to find the right service and connect with the right person.

What you can do here

  • Register as a customer or worker
  • Search workers by category and area
  • Request bookings with preferred dates
  • Track booking status from request to completion
  • Leave ratings after completed jobs
